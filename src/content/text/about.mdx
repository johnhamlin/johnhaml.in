My wife, Catherine, and I started Jellifish to make shopping for interiors clearer. We're starting with wallpaper. That means apples-to-apples pricing regardless of whether the manufacturer sells single rolls, double rolls, by the yard -- whatever -- and sorting products by how much it would cost to wallpaper your room. You shouldn't need to make a spreadsheet or have a friend with an interior decorator's license to wallpaper your powder room.

I've got an ever-growing org chart of AI agents handling product, design, engineering and more. I'm learning in public as I go and posting about it as [@johnhamlin](https://x.com/johnhamlin) on X.
